Patient Case Report

Patient ID: 49217-BZ
Patient name: Marko Juric
Date of birth: 14 March 1967
Attending physician: Dr. Ana Kovacevic, MD - Internal Medicine
Facility: St. Catherine Clinic, Zagreb
Visit date: 10 November 2027

Presenting complaint
Patient presents with recurrent morning headaches, intermittent dizziness and fatigue persisting over the last eleven weeks. Reports two episodes of blurred vision while at work. No chest pain, no shortness of breath at rest.

Vital signs
Blood pressure 162/98 mmHg (repeat 158/96 mmHg after ten minutes seated). Heart rate 88 bpm, regular. Temperature 36.7 C. Respiratory rate 16/min. Oxygen saturation 98% on room air. Weight 94.2 kg, height 178 cm, BMI 29.7.

Laboratory findings
Fasting glucose 7.4 mmol/L. HbA1c 6.8%. Total cholesterol 6.9 mmol/L, LDL 4.5 mmol/L, HDL 0.9 mmol/L, triglycerides 3.1 mmol/L. Serum creatinine 104 umol/L, eGFR 72 mL/min/1.73m2. Urinalysis positive for microalbuminuria (48 mg/g creatinine). Liver enzymes within reference range.

Assessment
Stage 2 hypertension with evidence of early hypertensive nephropathy. Metabolic syndrome: impaired glucose tolerance bordering on type 2 diabetes mellitus, mixed dyslipidaemia and central obesity. Estimated ten-year cardiovascular risk is high.

Plan
Start perindopril 5 mg once daily, titrate to 10 mg after four weeks if blood pressure remains above 140/90 mmHg. Start atorvastatin 20 mg nightly. Metformin 500 mg twice daily with meals. Referral to clinical dietitian and structured exercise programme. Home blood pressure diary. Repeat lipid panel, HbA1c and renal function in twelve weeks. Ophthalmology review for hypertensive retinopathy screening.

Notes
Patient consented to sharing of this record with the cardiology department. Insurance policy HR-4471-20885. Contact: marko.juric@example.hr, +385 91 442 7781. Record prepared by Dr. Ana Kovacevic, licence 118-4420.
